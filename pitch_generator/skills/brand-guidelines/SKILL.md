# House Brand Guidelines

## Palette
- Deep indigo (primary base)
- Warm amber (accent)
- Slate (neutral background)
- Terracotta (highlight and warmth)

## Light
Warm raking golden-hour light with long soft shadows and gentle natural falloff.

## Composition
Off-center hero subject with generous negative space and shallow depth of field following the rule of thirds.

## Subject
One realistic photographic subject crafted from authentic, handcrafted materials with natural textures.

## Never
No neon, fluorescent cyan or magenta colors, watermarks, logos, text overlays, harsh ring lights, or 3D renders.
